2027 hybrid platoon trust projections

Annual skill plus outcome trust supplies hitter hit and at-bat event rates (AVG) and every pitcher event rate. After-blend career plus outcome trust supplies hitter home runs, walks, strikeouts and total bases. Both start from platoon-specific skills, apply the matching split skill age curves before event conversion, and retain their separately refitted personal outcome effects.

Annual trust uses personal history when shrinking each annual skill measurement before its three-year blend. After-blend trust combines recent native skills first, then regresses that blend using older career evidence that excludes the recent three seasons. Skill estimates remain outcome-specific because each event equation has its own weights and predictors.

Future platoon park factors enter once on the neutral event forecasts. Event constraints apply within each matchup; counts are combined at the retained opponent shares before calculating AVG, SLG, OBP, OPS and pitcher ratios. SLG/OPS therefore use annual hits/AB with blended walks/TB. ERA uses combined pitcher events plus the matching annual neutral ERA personal correction. HBP/SF keep the existing overall history policy.

Hitter PA, non-platoon stolen-base rates and matchup shares retain their existing models. The supported SP workload model still determines TBF through expected starts and BF/start; IP and IP/start are rederived from the new out rates. Other pitchers retain their existing IP assumptions. Saves retain their separate rates and published reference workloads; ACE/SLAM and SD reference distributions stay fixed.

746 hitters and 1,016 pitchers have rate projections. Missing or zero workloads remain so. Projected skills show both hands and their weighted combination. Overall OPS and sortable OPS versus each pitcher hand are available. Runs and RBI appear by default in Rankings and Roster Builder, with sortable totals, R/RBI SDs and expanded sums. They reuse the existing conversion coefficients on the updated event inputs, with lineup and roster context held fixed, and scale to the chosen playing time. The full projection CSV includes both totals.

Historical evaluation compares earlier-only 2022–2026 forecasts with official next-season outcomes using the existing matched cohorts and harmonic sample weights. The hybrid improves weighted Spearman in all 13 outcomes versus the prior default, and RMSE in seven; some pitcher-rate and AVG errors increase. Method choice followed inspection of those years, so this is a retrospective comparison. The authenticated projection download includes native RMSE, R², correlation comparisons and paired player-history bootstrap intervals.

Coverage completion adds 179 supported player-role profiles (69 hitters and 110 pitchers), including players who missed the 2026 MLB season. Current roster and organization evidence now supplement the old participation screen. Available calendar-year skill histories are retained, with no invented 2026 observations. The existing 1,583 projections are unchanged. Added players receive the frozen skill, personal-effect, park, usage and workload models; R/RBI use the existing conversion fits. These workloads include missed-year and non-return risk and are not healthy-season guarantees. The added profiles do not constitute a new independent accuracy test.

Pitcher wins use the combined split-skill ERA/WHIP forecasts, a prior-league unearned-run allowance, innings per start, starter/relief innings and total IP. Each club receives a completed lineup under the existing free-agent, platoon and vacancy policies; its projected runs sum is divided by 162. Unknown future teams use a completed generic lineup, and option/FA scenarios are mixed after the nonlinear win conversion. Starter and relief win rates are fit separately on earlier seasons.

2022–2026 rolling forecasts cover 6,719 pitcher-seasons, including non-returners. Weighted win RMSE is 3.22 versus 3.26 for a recent win-rate baseline; weighted Spearman is 0.601 versus 0.612. This is a modest count-accuracy gain with weaker ranking performance. Historical team support uses prior-only scoring and dated preseason rosters; the current October FA policy lacks a retrospective contract snapshot test.

W and SV SDs standardize category totals (or per-IP rates in Quality view) against frozen eligible reference groups. They are fantasy grades, not prediction uncertainty. All-5 Sum SD includes K/ERA/WHIP/W/SV for pitchers and HR/SB/AVG/R/RBI for hitters; the earlier sums remain available. There are win totals for 985 pitchers with projected IP and save SD grades for the 386 pitchers with existing saves estimates. Unavailable saves stay unknown, so their all-5 sum stays blank. Existing saves, playing time and prior grades are retained. The full projection download adds W, W SD, SV and SV SD; native W uses unrounded IP, while display_W and the SD grades use displayed innings.
