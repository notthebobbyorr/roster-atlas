Platoon skill-aged · parks + outcome refit · 2027

Three-year skill blends receive split-specific skill aging before conversion to park-neutral hitter and pitcher event rates. Historical platoon-specific parks are removed before refitting personal outcome adjustments. Expected future platoon-specific parks enter once, after those adjustments. ERA is derived from the combined event counts and receives a separately refitted neutral-scale personal ERA correction.

Three prior seasons of league matchup counts, weighted 50%, 33⅓% and 16⅔%, supply the starting L/R/S-hitter and L/R-pitcher shares. Shrunk personal usage and projected relative ability adjust those shares. Total PA/IP is modeled separately, including zero-next-season-MLB cases. This update preserves the selected playing time, matchup shares, and non-platoon stolen-base totals.

The same selected model drives Rankings and Roster Builder. Projected playing time uses the new workload model; Direct and Ordered ballast retain the earlier workload alternatives with the selected outcome rates. Standard workloads remain a comparison view. The browser rounds PA to whole appearances and IP to the nearest out; the full-precision CSV is available beside the model selector.

1583 supported player-role projections: 677 hitters and 906 pitchers. Other player records remain available for saved rosters and dated role lists, with missing projections shown as unavailable. Saves retain the separately published rates and team budgets, using the selected innings in allocation weights. ACE/SLAM remain the independently published reference grades; old process-input blends are not presented as these new split-aged inputs.

Expected parks use the published 2027 schedule, prior opponent-hand frequencies, and saved roster destinations; unassigned players use an explicit prior-team proxy. Park selection has no effect on this variant because parks are already included. R, RBI and wins remain deferred. Switch hitters retain separate vs-LHP/vs-RHP usage branches.

In the 2022–2026 walk-forward comparison, weighted pitcher ERA RMSE improves from 1.6161 to 1.6091 and WHIP from .2550 to .2535; both rank correlations improve. Hitter results are mixed: AVG RMSE changes from .03151 to .03164. These are modest pitcher gains, not an across-the-board accuracy improvement.

All coefficient fits and rolling choices use earlier forecast seasons. These are reconstructed historical comparisons, not archived forecast vintages; model-design choices were made during the research. The downloadable model report gives features, targets, weighting, R², RMSE and limitations.
