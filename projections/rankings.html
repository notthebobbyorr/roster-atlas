ACE estimates overall pitching talent by combining Pitch Grade, Execution Grade, FA mph, FA VAA, SwStr%, Chase%, Ball%, and LA≤0%. Pitch Grade remains the separate stuff measure. ACE uses the same-season ERA− model applied to those projected skills. Higher is better: 50 is the frozen 2026 reference average and 10 points is one SD. Grades are uncapped. SLAM summarizes batting skills on the same grade scale. Both are independent of playing time and park selection and remain separate from Sum SD.

Process columns show regressed, recency-weighted inputs from the selected model. Pitch grade is the stuff grade on the 20–80 scale; execution is a separate metric. These input blends do not include the category-level age adjustment. Damage and SwStr are percentages; contact versus expected is in percentage points. Missing inputs remain blank in CSV and show — here.

IP/Start is a three-year MLB starter-only innings-per-start estimate using the selected playing-time recency weights, rounded to one decimal. No additional age or park adjustment; it does not change projected IP. No MLB starts in the window means unavailable.
