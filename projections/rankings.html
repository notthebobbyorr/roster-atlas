    Projection notes and help

    The flag beside a hitter identifies any prior-season or projected-season contact warning: whiff vs 95+ at least 32%, secondary whiff at least 38%, contact vs expected at most -6 percentage points, or SwStr at least 15%. Hover or focus for the triggering season and metrics. Qualified MLB observations take precedence; non-MLB observations are labeled. Projected SwStr uses a separate validated forecast. Missing measurements remain unevaluated. These warnings do not change projections or rankings.

    R/RBI contribution SD = projected total minus the fixed roster mean, divided by roster SD. Rate quality standardizes R/PA and RBI/PA separately, retaining lineup/team context. Missing R/RBI leaves the expanded hitter sum unavailable. Benchmarks stay fixed when filtering.

    One independent ranking per fantasy position, pooling all 30 teams. All P combines starters, relievers, and swingmen. All Hitters pools every hitting position; All Players combines both groups, with separate rows for a player’s hitting and pitching projections. Minor-league players with usable projections appear under their current FanGraphs MLB organization in the organization filter. They still have no projected MLB role and use neutral parks. Players with no known organization remain unassigned. Create named custom lists to compare any selection of pitchers or hitters. Includes RosterResource starting lineups, rotation, bullpen, and bench players assigned a starting lineup against either hand. CI = 1B/3B; MI = 2B/SS. UT-only means no eligible field position.

    ACE estimates overall pitching talent by combining Pitch Grade, Execution Grade, FA mph, FA VAA, SwStr%, Chase%, Ball%, and LA≤0%. Pitch Grade remains the separate stuff measure. ACE uses the same-season ERA− model applied to those projected skills. Higher is better: 50 is the frozen 2026 reference average and 10 points is one SD. Grades are uncapped. SLAM summarizes batting skills on the same grade scale. Both are independent of playing time and park selection and remain separate from Sum SD.

    Process columns show regressed, recency-weighted inputs from the selected model. Pitch grade is the stuff grade on the 20–80 scale; execution is a separate metric. These input blends do not include the category-level age adjustment. Damage and SwStr are percentages; contact versus expected is in percentage points. Missing inputs remain blank in CSV and show — here.

    IP/Start is a three-year MLB starter-only innings-per-start estimate using the selected playing-time recency weights, rounded to one decimal. No additional age or park adjustment; it does not change projected IP. No MLB starts in the window means unavailable.

    Reordering is a personal ranking; it does not change the projections. Projection rates and league benchmarks are the same frozen models retained projection snapshots. Current RR team supplies the park scenario. Starting-role lists are a dated roster observation, not a new forecast of 2027 roster decisions. Missing projections remain unavailable. The FA-aware playing-time option adds experimental R and RBI; wins remain unavailable. The original Sum SD excludes R/RBI; the expanded Sum SD adds their SD grades for hitters. Pitcher sums are unchanged. Saves use a separate role and projected-innings model, with a separate personal residual in the hybrid model; SV does not enter the three SD grades or Sum.
