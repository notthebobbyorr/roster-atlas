Starting-role rankings

Players with usable projections but no RosterResource role are included as Unassigned / FA, including unsigned players and prospects; this is not a confirmed free-agent designation. Custom lists

Check players across filters or lists, then send them to a custom list. In All Players, sending adds the selected players matching the destination’s pitcher/hitter group and keeps the other group selected. The table’s Select all checkbox selects or deselects the displayed players and keeps selections outside the current filters. Sending copies the players to the destination while you stay in your current view. Switch the List menu or use Open list when you are ready to reorder them.
