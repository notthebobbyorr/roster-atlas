Starting-role rankings

One independent ranking per fantasy position, pooling all 30 teams. Includes RosterResource starting lineups, rotation, bullpen, and bench players assigned a starting lineup against either hand. CI = 1B/3B; MI = 2B/SS. UT-only means no eligible field position.

Drag rows on desktop, use ↑ / ↓ on any device, or enter a rank. Rankings are saved on this device for your account and shared between projection models. Export/import a backup to transfer them to another device.

Reordering is a personal ranking; it does not change the projections. Projection rates and league benchmarks are the same frozen models used by Skill Atlas. Current RR team supplies the park scenario. Starting-role lists are a dated roster observation, not a new forecast of 2027 roster decisions. Missing projections remain unavailable. R, RBI, wins and saves remain deferred in these skill models.
