sStats · 2027 pitcher ERA & WHIP

WHIP uses a blend of independent Atlas and measured-MLB sStats forecasts for hits, walks and outs. Where the MLB forecast is supported, sStats receives about 27% of the weight. Sparse histories use Atlas. ERA retains the 17-skill platoon forecast with Atlas history backfill. The displayed WHIP, hit and walk rates use the blended components. Workload, wins and saves use Complete Projections 11.13.1.
