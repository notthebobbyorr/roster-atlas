Expected home runs on 20°+ contact

xHR/FB% estimates the chance that contact at 20° or above becomes a home run. The estimate uses 2024–2026 tracked contact scored with the park-aware model, weighted toward recent seasons and regressed toward the recent league rate. The contact history reflects the parks where the player hit or pitched. Players without that history use the league conversion estimate.

xHR (20°+) multiplies projected plate appearances or batters faced by the projected contact rate, the 20°+ share and the expected conversion rate. Hitters use the 20°+ share from Atlas's HR skill forecast. Pitcher shares come from recent tracked contact with league shrinkage. Pitcher xHR measures home runs allowed. Playing-time changes scale xHR at the displayed rate.
